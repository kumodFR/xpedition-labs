Our Purpose

Turning scientific possibilities into agricultural products.

Agriculture is entering a new era of innovation.

Advances in biotechnology, genetics, breeding and crop science are creating powerful new possibilities. At the same time, farmers and agricultural companies continue to seek better solutions for productivity, resilience and sustainable crop production.

Xpedition Labs connects these opportunities.

We identify meaningful agricultural needs, define the product opportunity and bring together the science, technology, intellectual property and partnerships required to develop commercially relevant solutions.

From opportunity to product, we provide the direction that connects science with the market.

Focus Areas

Building solutions for important agricultural needs.

Our development interests are centred on areas where advanced crop science can create meaningful value for farmers and the agricultural industry.

Crop Productivity

Innovations that enable crops to realise greater and more consistent production potential.

Crop Protection
